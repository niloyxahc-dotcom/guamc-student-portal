1st Prof
  • Focus on Gross Anatomy viscera attachments and blood supply.

Physiology & Biochemistry (Munafeul Aza)

1st Prof
  • Core physiological systems: CVS, Renal mechanisms, Endocrinology.
  • Biochemistry: Carbohydrate & lipid metabolic pathways.
  • Practical viva: Hematology experiments, ESR, and blood indices.

Ilmul Advia / Dravyaguna (Pharmacognosy)

Core Specialty
  • Medicinal plants: Botanical classification, Mizaj/temperament, active constituents.
  • Practical identification: Crude drug specimens and herbarium sheets.
  • Formulations, compounding, and therapeutic dosage calculations.

Bedside Clinical Methods

Clinical Viva
  • Systematic General Physical Examination (GPE) checklists.
  • Pulse examination according to classical principles (Nabz/Nadi).
  • Case presentation methodology and structured clinical history taking.
